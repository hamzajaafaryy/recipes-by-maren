## Indexing and publishing rules

- Keep a category available in the CMS even when it has no published recipes.
- Show categories in the public directory only after at least one real, published recipe is assigned.
- Category archives with fewer than 3 published recipes use noindex,follow and are excluded from the XML sitemap. Reassess once the archive has at least 3 substantial, relevant recipes.
- Do not create near-duplicate categories or publish empty archive pages just to target more keywords.
- Keep one primary search topic per category. Recipe pages should target their own specific dish query, not repeat the broad category keyword.
- Preserve existing category URLs. If a slug ever changes, add a permanent redirect and update internal links and the sitemap.

## Recipe publishing standard

Before a recipe is published, verify:
1. The recipe was actually cooked and the ingredients, quantities, temperatures, timing, and serving yield are accurate.
2. The page has original, clear food photography, a descriptive filename, and useful alt text.
3. The recipe has a unique title, concise meta description, useful introduction, complete ingredients, numbered instructions, prep/cook/total time, servings, and relevant categories.
4. Internal links point to relevant category and related recipe pages.
5. Recipe structured data reflects visible page content. Never invent ratings, reviews, nutrition, author experience, or testing claims.

## Pinterest publishing standard

- Create vertical 2:3 pins (for example, 1000 × 1500 px) with a clear, readable recipe title and a genuine photo of the finished dish.
- Use a distinct pin title and description that naturally mention the dish and a useful angle (quick dinner, easy prep, family meal, etc.).
- Link each pin directly to its matching recipe page, not the homepage or a category archive.
- Add relevant board placement and descriptive image alt text. Avoid keyword stuffing and repeated near-identical pins.
- Only pin a recipe after its website page is complete and its photo represents the actual finished recipe.
